## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment** — Product-specific structure, partly interchangeable skin. The IA, data model, and copy are genuinely authored for this product: a roadmap-first PIC workflow with cross-check "Siap berangkat / Pulang tuntas", wallet flow (Bank/QRIS/Kas/E-Money), budget-vs-realisasi, and SPPD lifecycle. An unrelated product could not reuse it unchanged. However the visual layer is category-generic: big black stat cards with oversized display numbers, a red-on-white accent, pill navigation, uppercase micro-labels, and a hero with a huge percentage are the default "bold dashboard" look.

## Overall Impression

A deeply considered, domain-true tool that is doing too much on one screen. The data model and derived finance logic are the strongest part; the biggest opportunity is turning the 8-section work screen into a guided, progressive flow instead of a wall of tables and forms.

## What's Working

- **Authentic workflow modelling.** Cross-check verdicts (`berangkatOk`/`pulangOk`), `epAnomali`, and budget-vs-realisasi derivation encode real PIC judgement, not generic CRUD. `store.js` financeSummary is the product's spine.
- **A coherent small visual vocabulary.** Wallet badges, `doc-chip`, status badges, and section numbers reuse consistently across list, print, and detail views.
- **Honest prototype signalling.** The "Prototype / localStorage" banner and `// FUTURE: GAS` markers set accurate expectations.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] One screen, everything at once.** The work screen stacks hero progress + up to 6 check chips + "Cek Persiapan" (24 items) + an 8-pill flowbar + 8 numbered sections with tables/forms. Why it matters: no single focus; users scanning for "what do I do next" drown in chrome. Fix: default the first-run experience to one active section with the rest collapsed, surface a single "next action" CTA, and move the master checklist behind a step. Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`
- **[P1] Navigation taxonomy confusion.** Eight top-level nav items (Dashboard, Event & Tugas, Arsip, Produk, Master Barang, Rekap Global, Panduan, Pengaturan) exceed the ≤5 rule, and "Produk" vs "Master Barang" are near-indistinguishable ("produk dagangan" vs "barang bawaan"). The legacy-tab map also funnels nine old hashes into one `kerja` tab. Why it matters: Jordan cannot predict where anything lives; even the FAQ has to explain the difference. Fix: split the nav into work vs catalogs, rename to "Produk Dagangan" / "Barang Bawaan", and reconsider the 8-item top bar. Suggested command: `/impeccable shape`
- **[P2] No undo; full re-render loses context.** Every action calls `A.reload()`, so a toggle mid-page resets scroll and any unsaved fields. Destructive deletes offer only a confirm dialog — no undo. Why it matters: Alex loses place on a long screen; a mis-click on 24 checklist items is laborious to reverse. Fix: targeted re-render for toggles, optimistic undo toasts for deletes, preserve scroll. Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`
- **[P2] Accessibility gaps.** The modal has `role="dialog"` but no `aria-modal`, focus trap, or focus return; active nav is a color class with no `aria-current`; many `<label class="label">` have no `for=`; status is often conveyed by color alone (green/red badges). Why it matters: Sam cannot complete the flow keyboard/screen-reader-only, and focus escapes the modal. Fix: add `aria-modal` + trap + restore, `aria-current="page"`, explicit `for/id` and `aria-labelledby`, and text/icon parity for color states. Suggested command: `/impeccable audit`
- **[P3] Generic dashboard skin.** Plus Jakarta Sans (detector-flagged) plus the black card / red accent / giant-number hero pattern reads as interchangeable AI-dashboard styling rather than a distinct SPPD identity. Why it matters: the product-specific soul in the copy and IA is undercut by a familiar skin. Fix: pick a more characterful body face and give the display numbers a deliberate treatment tied to the domain. Suggested command: `/impeccable typeset`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User)** — No keyboard shortcuts anywhere; the 24-item checklist must be toggled one at a time; each toggle re-renders the whole page and resets scroll. Forms require explicit "Simpan" per section with no batch save. High friction for a daily-driver tool.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent)** — Modal opens without focus trap and focus is not restored on close (`ui.js:72`); active nav item is signalled by `bg-ink` only (app.js:20), no `aria-current`; several form labels lack `for`/`id` association (e.g. `pages.js:150`, `pages2.js:110`); status meaning relies on red/green badges alone (e.g. `pages2.js:447`). Zoom/keyboard-only completion of the work screen is unreliable.

**Jordan (First-Timer)** — "Produk" vs "Master Barang", "Aset Jual" vs "Aset & Alat", and "rekap tuntas"/"oversold" require reading the FAQ. The first meaningful action is buried: Dashboard → Event & Tugas → open event → pick section, with no single obvious "start here".

## Minor Observations

- Two progress-bar implementations exist (`ui.progress` and local `progressBar` in pages.js/pages2.js) — unify.
- `tabRoadmap` (pages.js) duplicates the checklist blocks already built by `ckSection` (pages2.js).
- STATUS_MAP keeps both `berjalan`→"Berjalan" and `aktif`→"Berjalan", which can mislead status filters.
- Print output (`printHTML`) is clean and print-specific — a genuine strength worth keeping in refactors.

## Questions to Consider

- What if the work screen opened on exactly one "next action" instead of eight sections?
- Does "Produk" and "Master Barang" need to be two destinations, or one catalog with two tabs?
- What would a confident, domain-distinct version of the stat cards look like — must they be black?
